Privacy Policy for Addis lens

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Addis lens ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location Information: Access to your device's precise (GPS) location is required to calculate distances to nearby buildings and accurately display Augmented Reality (AR) location points.
  • Camera Permission: Camera access is used to display live camera feeds for AR building scanning and to enable building managers/owners to upload profile and shop photos. Camera feeds are processed locally on your device for real-time AR display and are not recorded or saved without user action.
  • Account Information: Building managers and shop owners provide an email address for authentication and profile management.

2. How Information is Used

The collected data is strictly used to:

  • Identify nearby buildings and present AR overlays.
  • Manage partner building profiles and shop directory listings.
  • Authenticate authorized building managers and shop owners.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal information or location data to third parties. All network communication between the app and cloud backend (Firebase, Cloudinary, Groq AI) is encrypted via HTTPS/SSL.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
